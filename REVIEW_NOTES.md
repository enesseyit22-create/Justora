# App Review notes

Justora is a focus timer and a short daily list for people who struggle to start. It does not treat, diagnose, or give medical advice.

No login is required. There is no account, so there is no account to delete. Lists, timers, and settings stay on the device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## How to test

1. Open the app. A three-step guide explains the list, Start, and points. Skip lands on Today.
2. Free use does not require a purchase: add a task, tap Start, and run the 25-minute length or the 2-minute Just start.
3. The paywall opens from Progress (Justora Pro), from a locked timer length, or from a locked theme in Settings. The close control is the × at the top left. Not now is at the bottom.
4. On the paywall, the price and period come from StoreKit when the products are loaded. If they are not loaded, the screen says the store shows the price before payment and that the plan renews until cancelled. It does not invent a trial. A trial line appears only when the product includes one.
5. Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings. Terms of Use and the Apple Standard EULA are linked on the paywall. Privacy Policy is linked on the paywall and in Settings.
6. Notification permission is not requested at launch. It is offered only while a timer is running, or when a reminder is turned on in Settings. Denying it leaves the timer usable.
7. The home screen name is Justora, the same name as the binary.
