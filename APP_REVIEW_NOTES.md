Justora is a visual focus timer and a short daily list. It does not provide medical advice and does not treat or diagnose any condition.

No account is required. The app works offline. Data stays on the device except for App Store purchases (Apple and RevenueCat) and, on a release build, optional JavaScript updates from Expo.

How to try it:

1. Open the app. Three short screens lead into a two-minute timer. That is the first session.
2. After it starts, Continue opens the subscription screen. Not now returns to the app. Restore purchases is on that screen and in Settings.
3. Today holds up to three tasks on the free tier. Tap a task to start its timer. Just start begins two minutes from Today.
4. Stats shows the streak, a weekly recap, badges, and (with Pro) history and estimate comparisons.
5. Settings has sound, haptics, check-ins, the haptic metronome (off by default), notification reminders, themes, and language. Notification permission is not requested at launch. It is offered the first time a timer is running.

Subscriptions, once products are live: Justora Pro, monthly `focus_pro_monthly` and yearly `focus_pro_yearly`, entitlement `pro`, 7-day free trial configured on the App Store products. The paywall shows the StoreKit price, period, trial (only if the product includes a free intro offer), and auto-renewal terms, plus Terms and Privacy.

Widgets and Live Activities appear on an App Store or development build, not in Expo Go. Add the Justora widget from the widget gallery while a timer is running to see the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
